What is the Fortune Intern Network?

FIN is a professional growth platform and career ecosystem bridging academic education and industry readiness for tertiary students across Africa. Centralized online at fortuneintern.com, it connects campus talent directly with corporate networks, industry leaders and institutional partners.

Practical skillsLeadershipMentorshipBridging Dreams and Careers

Mission

Empower tertiary students through structured technical training, practical leadership experience, public speaking mastery and direct workplace integration.

Vision

Build a unified, inter university ecosystem of highly skilled, employment ready and entrepreneurial graduates capable of competing globally.

Governance alignment

All chapter activities, leadership selections and operational protocols strictly comply with the FIN Campus Chapter Constitution.

Advisory Oversight & Corporate Reporting

Chapter Patron

A designated senior academic faculty member (Professor, Department Head or Dean of Students) who provides institutional guidance, academic backing and administrative oversight to the chapter.

Monthly reporting protocol

Operational and financial reports are submitted to the FIN Corporate Team by the 5th of every month, without exception.

Constitutional compliance

Leadership selection, discipline and program delivery follow the FIN Campus Chapter Constitution; disputes escalate to the Patron then to FIN Corporate.

Executive check ins

Monthly executive meetings minuted by the General Secretary and filed with chapter records and corporate reports.

Tiered Communication Architecture

TIER 1 · EXECUTIVE

Slack Workspaces & Microsoft Teams. Corporate team, campus ambassadors and chapter execs: directives, monthly report submissions, executive meetings and the constitutional file repository.

TIER 2 · CHAPTER

WhatsApp Group & WhatsApp Channel. Group for execs and active members: coordination and collaboration. Channel for one way official broadcasts, urgent notices, event registration links and weekly announcements without clutter.

TIER 3 · PUBLIC

fortuneintern.com, LinkedIn, Instagram, X and Discord. Opportunity publishing, applications, resource downloads, achievement spotlights, media campaigns, live updates and cross campus study and mentorship hubs.

Rule: official notices flow top down from Tier 1 to Tier 3; member feedback flows bottom up through the General Secretary. No chapter announcement is valid outside these channels.

SOP 01 · Meetings & Minutes

  • Frequency: executive meeting at least once a month; general member meeting once a semester.
  • Quorum: two thirds of the executive board must be present for decisions to bind the chapter.
  • Agenda: circulated by the General Secretary at least 48 hours before the meeting on Tier 1.
  • Minutes: recorded live, approved at the next sitting, signed by the General Secretary and President.

Filing

Approved minutes are stored in the chapter records folder and attached to the monthly corporate report.

Attendance

The Deputy General Secretary logs attendance; three unexcused absences trigger a formal check in.

SOP 02 · Reporting & Records

  • Deadline: operational and financial reports reach FIN Corporate by the 5th of every month.
  • Contents: membership count, programs run, placements, finances, media output and challenges.
  • Channel: submitted through the Tier 1 workspace; a copy is filed in chapter records.
  • Late reports: flagged to the Patron and the Corporate operations lead after the 7th.

Record retention

Minutes, reports and financial records are kept for the full academic year and handed over at transition.

Verification

The Vice President / Operations Lead cross checks figures before the President signs the submission.

SOP 03 · Finance & Dues

  • Budget: a semester budget is drafted by the Financial Secretary and approved by the executive board.
  • Receipts: every inflow and outflow is receipted and logged in the chapter ledger.
  • Dual sign off: expenditures require the Financial Secretary and the President (or Patron above threshold).
  • Dues: member dues are tracked per person; arrears are reported privately, never publicly shamed.

Audit

A financial disclosure accompanies every monthly report; the Patron may request a full audit at any time.

Transparency

Members may inspect the ledger through the Financial Secretary on request.

SOP 04 · Recruitment & Onboarding

  • Window: the one month onboarding phase opens each academic year.
  • Channels: campus wide campaign directing students to fortuneintern.com and the official WhatsApp channels.
  • Registration: every member registers on the portal and is added to the Tier 2 group.
  • Induction: orientation covers the constitution, the three programs, the brand and the code of conduct.

Membership register

The General Secretary maintains the register; the Deputy tracks attendance and engagement.

Kit

New members receive the branded kit and are photographed for the chapter gallery.

SOP 05 · Events & Media

  • Proposal: event concept, budget and date submitted to the executive board two weeks ahead.
  • Approval: President approves; Patron notified for institutional events.
  • Creative: Graphic Designer produces assets; Media Head reviews; President signs off before publication.
  • Coverage: Media Head captures photos and video; highlights published on Tier 3 within 48 hours.

Brand compliance

Only approved logo lockups, navy and gold palette, and the motto on every creative.

Archive

Event photos, flyers and reports are archived in chapter records and the gallery.

SOP 06 · Discipline & Escalation

  • Step 1: private verbal warning by the President or General Secretary, logged in minutes.
  • Step 2: written warning with a corrective action plan and a review date.
  • Step 3: suspension of duties pending Patron review for repeated or serious breaches.
  • Step 4: removal from office or membership per the constitution, with Corporate notified.

Fair process

The member is heard at every step; decisions are documented and communicated in writing.

Escalation

Disputes between officers escalate to the Patron, then to FIN Corporate for final resolution.
